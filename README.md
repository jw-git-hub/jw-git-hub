- **For a site you already have** — SEO audit and optimisation, readiness for AI assistants, loading speed, analytics of clicks to messengers, a quiz before the first message.
- **Telegram** — bots for requests, catalogue, booking and payment with an admin panel in the messenger; channels and groups that post on their own; a menu and price list inside a channel.
- **Automation** — one price list for the site and social media, autoposting to Instagram and Facebook, data collection from sites, feeds and chats, linking CRM, spreadsheets and reminders.

After launch you choose: take everything and run it yourself, or leave maintenance with me. Code, domain and accounts are yours either way.
